I am a Research Scientist at [Singapore Management University](http://www.mysmu.edu/faculty/sfcheng/), working on reinforcement learning for LLM reasoning and agents. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from SMU, an M.S. in Management Science and Engineering from [Jilin University](https://www.jlu.edu.cn/index.htm#), and a B.S. in Information Management and Information Systems from [Nanjing Agricultural University](https://english.njau.edu.cn/mainm.htm).

## Research Focus

My work connects sequential decision-making, post-training, and inference-time control. Current directions include:

* **LLM reasoning and agents:** search-augmented reasoning, agentic AI, and adaptive inference.
* **Policy learning:** PPO, GRPO, GAIL, and offline RL for reasoning control.
* **Optimization:** constrained RL, Lagrangian and primal-dual methods, MILP, and Bayesian optimization.

## Selected Research

### Early Rumor Detection: Learning When a Frozen LLM Should Decide

Co-first-author work accepted at KDD 2026. A lightweight imitation-learning policy learns when to stop reading while balancing accuracy, earliness, and stability.
